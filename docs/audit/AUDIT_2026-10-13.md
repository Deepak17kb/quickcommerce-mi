# Audit of 2026-10-13 against the master checklist

Scope: the owner's "audit, fix and validate" checklist, applied to the project as it stood after the additions of the
same day. Most of the checklist was already in place and is listed with its evidence, not rebuilt. Two things were not
in place and were fixed; one new defect was found in the source data. Nothing in `data/raw` was written: its sha256
baseline (`scripts/verify_raw_integrity.py`) passes in the run of 2026-10-13.

## 1. What inspecting the source workbook showed

File inspected, read-only: `Blinkit_Master_FINAL_Clean_v2.xlsx` in the owner's `Downloads/` (sha256 `568313ec…77b696`,
checked 2026-10-13). `Blinkit_REAL_v7.xlsx` is no longer at the path CLAUDE.md records, so it was not inspected.

**Store names and addresses are a public store map.** Every store carries `source = QuickCommerceMap public store
listings`, `source_type = public_web_observation`, `location_accuracy = approx. 50–100 m`. That provenance is about
where a store is.

**How a product was tied to a store is not recorded anywhere.** `MASTER_PRODUCTS` has one row per listing with a
`store_id`; `STORE_PRODUCT_MAP` repeats that pairing, 21,949 rows, one store per row. The provenance columns on those
rows are copies of the store's own. No column says the product was seen in that store, and no sheet describes a method.

Together with the statistical audit (`docs/STORE_LABEL_AUDIT_v1.md`: the label cannot be told apart from a random
one) the product–store link is of **unknown provenance in the source itself**. Status: UNVERIFIED. This does not show
the collection was invalid; it shows the file cannot support store-level claims.

**New defect: 27 REAL prices were not observed in the snapshot.** The workbook marks 27 rows `Web researched` with a
URL: 14 from blinkit.com product pages, 13 from other sites (Patanjali, Kapiva, HamroBazaar, AapkaBazar, an Amazon
price-history page, Zepto, BigBasket, Neel Ayurvedics, The Wellness Shop, PharmEasy). `data/raw` labels all 27
`price_provenance = observed`. 0.12% of rows, but thirteen of them are not Blinkit prices at all.

## 6. Unresolved, and the data each needs

1. **Product–store link.** Needs the collection record for the store column, or a fresh per-store observation.
2. **Matcher accuracy.** Needs human labels on the 297-pair review page (0 today).
3. **Every dynamic model.** Needs a real panel; `docs/COLLECTION_PLANNER_v1.md` sizes it.
4. **Competitors.** No Instamart, Flipkart Minutes or BigBasket row is real. Zepto is not in the project.
5. **27 web-researched prices.** Need re-observation, or raw patch v3 to relabel them (with the pack-quantity defect).
6. **What the source's ETA field is.** It follows the product record, not the store.
7. **Version control.** The project is not a git repository, so code changes have no history or backup.
